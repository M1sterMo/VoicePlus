VoicePlus is a free, open-source player for audiobooks stored on your device. Choose your audiobook folders with Android's file picker, then listen offline without creating an account.

PLAYBACK

- Remembers your position in each book
- Adjustable playback speed and bookmarks
- Volume boost, with an option to remember it across all books
- Sleep timer with end-of-chapter options and interaction reset
- Customizable playback toolbar, lock-screen controls and media-button actions
- Android Auto and a resizable home-screen widget

MAKE YOUR LIBRARY YOURS

- Book-inspired portrait covers, Grid or List view, and two or three books per row
- Personal shelves and manually grouped series with editable book order
- Current keeps in-progress books easy to reach, even inside a series
- Edit titles and covers without rewriting audio files; preserve edits through rescans and portable backups
- Listening log and statistics calculated on your device
- Character notes for each book
- Edit chapter names and fix incorrect chapter numbering
- Hide books without deleting their files
- Back up and restore your progress and settings

PRIVACY

VoicePlus has no account, ads, analytics or crash-reporting service. Your library and listening history are stored locally. If Android system backup is enabled, eligible app data may be backed up by your configured provider. The optional online cover search sends a book title and author query to DuckDuckGo; opening a result downloads an image from its host. Cover search only runs when you choose to use it.

Privacy policy: https://github.com/mistermo-vibecode/VoicePlus/blob/main/PRIVACY.md
